What we do

Four practice verticals

Every project sits in one of our four verticals. Each is led by a Sector Head who owns project quality, member development, and sector expertise in that domain.

01

Consumer Products

We use data analysis to build models that explain consumer behavior and shape targeted marketing strategies. We advise on sustainable practices, pricing, and more, and we work with businesses of all sizes.

Typical engagements
  • Market sizing
  • Consumer segmentation
  • Pricing strategy
  • Brand positioning
  • Go-to-market planning
Sector Head Savannah Stewart
02

Healthcare

We bring insight into global health and the latest technological advances in the industry. Our team works closely with providers and organizations on complex problems and delivers customized solutions that drive innovation.

Typical engagements
  • Payer/provider strategy
  • Digital health
  • Patient journey mapping
  • Regulatory pathway analysis
  • Life sciences market entry
Sector Head Ava Meisel
03

Technology, Media & Telecommunications

Businesses have to keep innovating to stay ahead, and understanding the digital space is central to growth today. We help TMT clients reach their full potential.

Typical engagements
  • Digital transformation strategy
  • Product-market fit
  • Platform business models
  • Media landscape advisory
Sector Head Adam Peocz
04

Real Assets & Public Policy

We work where capital-intensive assets meet public policy: real estate strategy, infrastructure development, and the regulatory forces that shape both. From housing markets to energy infrastructure to sovereign investment, we help clients make complex, long-horizon decisions.

Typical engagements
  • Market entry & site selection
  • Infrastructure feasibility
  • Housing policy
  • Public-private partnerships
  • Urban development
  • Zoning & land use
Sector Head Melvin Ackah

For organizations

What a TCC team delivers

We partner with startups, growing companies, and established organizations on semester-long projects. You get a dedicated team of Princeton students, led by an Engagement Manager and overseen by a Sector Head, focused on a question that matters to your business.

Deliverables

The work we produce

  • Market analysis & sizing

    Bottom-up TAM, SAM, and SOM estimates, customer segmentation, and the research behind every number so you can defend it.

  • Go-to-market strategy

    Which customers to target first, how to price and position the offer, and a launch plan your team can act on.

  • Competitive landscape

    A structured view of competitors and adjacent players, how you compare, and where the openings are.

  • Growth & expansion

    New markets, products, and partnerships, with a recommendation on where to place your next bet.

  • Product & user research

    Customer interviews, surveys, and analysis that test product-market fit and show what users actually need.

  • Investor & partner materials

    Pitch decks, investor and acquirer target lists, and the storyline that ties your numbers together.

What you can expect

Built for your decision

A dedicated team

An Engagement Manager as your single point of contact, plus a team of analysts on your project for the semester.

Sector expertise

Every project sits in one of our four verticals, and its Sector Head reviews the final work before it reaches you.

Client-ready output

A polished final deck and presentation to your team, with the analysis and sources behind it.

Confidentiality

We never share your name or project details publicly without your written approval.

For students

How members grow

Everyone starts as an Analyst, regardless of prior experience. Advancement depends on performance and approval.

1

Analyst

Entry tier. Train in the Fall; staffable on projects from Spring.

2

Associate

2+ semesters, an EM or Sector Head recommendation, and Executive Board approval.

3

Engagement Manager

Leads a single project as the client-facing contact. Associates and above.

4

Partner

2+ completed projects, proven mentorship, and a unanimous Executive Board vote.

The year

Train in the Fall, deliver in the Spring

Fall · Train

  • Analysts complete onboarding and training
  • Associates and Partners run live projects
  • Every Analyst presents a capstone deck to a Partner panel

Spring · Deliver

  • All tiers eligible to be staffed
  • Trained Analysts join client teams
  • Up to four concurrent projects

Recruiting

Apply to TCC

We recruit once a year, at the start of the Fall semester. No prior consulting experience is required. Curiosity and how you think matter most.

  1. Weeks 1–2
    Applications open. Submit your resume.
  2. Weeks 2–3
    Video application, then a case and behavioral interview.
  3. End of Week 3
    Decisions are sent to all applicants.
  4. Before Week 5
    Onboarding for new Analysts, then Fall training begins.
